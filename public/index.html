On touch devices, use the on-screen stick and action buttons.

Goal

Win two of up to three rounds. Win a round by K.O. or by leading in HP when time runs out.

Fighting

Your crosshair turns red when an attack can reach. Attacks use stamina.

Block

Hold block to stop hits from the front. A shield blocks much more damage.

Parry

When the PARRY ring appears, click as it closes while facing your opponent to stagger them.

Weapons and items

Find fists, a sword, spear, shield or potion. Walk over items to pick them up; use F to swap and G to drop.

Special

Your SP bar fills when you take damage, block or parry. Press E when it is full.

Details

Practice: Choose a bot level for solo practice. Choose Knight, Barbarian or Rogue for your fighter.

Match: each fighter has 100 HP. Matches are best of three 90-second rounds. Win each round by K.O. or lead in HP when time ends; equal HP gives neither player the round. A disconnect gives the other fighter the win.

Move: WASD moves at 6 m/s and the mouse looks. Space jumps at 6.5 m/s. Shift dashes at 16 m/s for 0.18 seconds, costs 25 stamina, and has a 0.7-second cooldown. Stamina holds 100, refills at 24 per second, and starts refilling 0.9 seconds after you spend it. Swinging slows you to 75%; blocking to 50%; a stun to 45%.

Attack: left click to swing and to try a parry. Fists deal 7 damage at 1.1 m reach, with a 0.16-second wind-up and 0.48-second swing. A sword deals 20 damage at 1.9 m reach, with a 0.30-second wind-up and 0.72-second swing. A spear deals 25 damage at 2.9 m reach and a straight strip: the target centre must be in front, within 0.7 m of the thrust line and no farther than 3.3 m, with a 0.40-second wind-up and 0.95-second swing. Weapons use stamina. A hit lands after its wind-up if your opponent is in front and in range.

Parry: attack in the final 0.22 seconds before a hit lands, while facing its attacker within 30 degrees. Each attempt costs 15 stamina and has a 0.6-second cooldown; success refunds the cost, prevents damage, and staggers the attacker for 1 second. For 5 seconds, their DEF is 0 and your hits deal 20% extra damage. A mistimed try costs stamina.

Block: hold right mouse or C to block attacks from within 75 degrees in front of you. A block starts after you hold it for 0.12 seconds. A shield reduces damage by 85% and costs 0.8 stamina per damage stopped; without one, it reduces 40% and costs 1.5 per damage stopped. If you cannot pay, you take the hit and suffer a 0.7-second guard break. A carried shield also gives 35 DEF.

Special: Your SP bar gains 2 points per HP of damage you take, 4 per blocked hit and 35 per parry, up to 100. When full, press E to power up for 0.35 seconds, then use Double Strike with a sword (two 20-damage slashes, each parryable), Lunge with a spear (dash 3 m and thrust for 40 damage), or Shield Bash with a shield and no weapon (22 damage, 0.5-second stun, 1.6 m reach). With fists only, E says “No special: hold a weapon or a shield”; if SP is not full, it says “SPECIAL NOT READY”.

Items: the first drop is 1–4 seconds into the fight, with matching items at mirrored spots. Later drops come every 7–16 seconds, sometimes two at once, at least 3 m from fighters. Walk within 1.6 m of a landed item to pick it up. Swords, shields and spears are equally likely. If your main hand is full, press F near a dropped weapon to swap; your old weapon drops where you stand. From halfway through the match, potions can drop: they restore 30 HP (up to 100) and make attacks 35% faster for 8 seconds. A light beam marks each drop for 3 seconds before it lands.

Controls: WASD move · Mouse look · Left click attack/parry · Right click or C block · Space jump · Shift dash · F pick up/swap · G drop · E special · Esc pause.
